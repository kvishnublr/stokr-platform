## 🔄 Signal Generation Pipeline

### **Stage 1: Market Data Collection**
- Polls `MarketDataQueryService` for latest 1-minute candles
- Collects from NIFTY_50 (or configurable universe group)
- Fetches last 120 candles per symbol (~2 hours of data)
- **Frequency**: Every 45 seconds (configurable: `stokr.adv-dashboard.feed-interval-ms`)

### **Stage 2: Setup Detection**
Detects setups like:
- **S3S7**: Supply/Demand zone reversals
- **S7RangeFade**: Range consolidation breaks
- **S3VWAP**: VWAP rejection patterns
- **IndexHunt**: Index-driven momentum plays
- Plus custom detectors based on volume, ATR, regime

### **Stage 3: Quality Scoring**
Scores are based on:
- **Pattern Strength**: How clear/validated the setup is
- **Market Regime**: TRENDING_UP/DOWN, CHOPPY, VOLATILE, QUIET
- **Volume/Volatility**: ATR and volume confirmation
- **Risk/Reward**: Entry to target vs entry to SL

**Score Range**: 0.0 to 1.0 (displayed as 0-100 on dashboard)
- **80+**: Premium quality setups (A+ SETUP)
- **60-79**: Good quality setups (B SETUP)
- **Below 60**: Lower confidence (not shown in Signal Intelligence)

### **Stage 4: Ranking & Display**
- Top 23 setups ranked by:
  1. Source rank (live market > audit)
  2. Status rank (EXECUTED > OMS_ELIGIBLE > WATCHLIST)
  3. Activity score (descending)
  4. AI score/confidence (descending)
- **Update frequency**: Every 45 seconds

## 🔧 Configuration

**Service that generates these signals:**

```yaml
# application.yml
stokr:
  adv-dashboard:
    universe-group: NIFTY_50         # Which stocks to scan
    max-symbols: 50                  # How many to process
    feed-interval-ms: 45000          # Poll every 45 seconds
    mover-cache-ms: 12000            # Cache live movers
    mover-scan-limit: 80             # Top movers to track
```

**Scheduling:**
- `AdvIntelligenceFeedService.refreshFromMarketData()` 
  - `@Scheduled(fixedDelayString = "${stokr.adv-dashboard.feed-interval-ms:45000}")`
  - Runs every 45 seconds during NSE session
